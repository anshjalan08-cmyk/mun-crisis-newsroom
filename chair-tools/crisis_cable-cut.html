ARTICLE — tws-0218

Two submarine cables to Matsu severed within nine minutes; Taipei convenes emergency session

OUTLET: tws (Trident Wire Service)

TIME: 2026-08-05T02:18:00Z

BYLINE: Trident Wire Service

DATELINE: TAIPEI

TAGS: breaking

HEADLINE: Two submarine cables to Matsu severed within nine minutes; Taipei convenes emergency session

STANDFIRST: Chunghwa Telecom confirms total loss of service to the Matsu Islands. A vessel broadcasting no identification was recorded over both cable routes in the preceding hour.

BODY

Both remaining submarine communications cables linking the Matsu Islands to Taiwan's main island were severed within a nine-minute window early Wednesday, cutting all fixed-line internet and telephone service to a population of approximately 13,000.

Chunghwa Telecom said the Taiwan-Matsu No. 2 cable failed at 01:41 local time and the Taiwan-Matsu No. 3 cable at 01:50. The company described both as 'complete breaks' rather than degradations, and said a repair vessel could not be on station before 19 August at the earliest.

Taiwan's Ministry of Digital Affairs has activated microwave backup, restoring what officials called 'emergency-grade' capacity at roughly two per cent of normal bandwidth. Priority has been assigned to hospital, coast guard and air traffic control traffic.

Automatic Identification System records reviewed by TWS show one vessel transiting both cable corridors between 00:55 and 01:48. The vessel was broadcasting no identifying signal. TWS has not established its flag, ownership or destination, and cannot confirm any connection to the cable failures.

QUOTE: We are not in a position this morning to tell you that this was deliberate. We are also not in a position to tell you that it was an accident. What I can tell you is that two cables do not usually fail nine minutes apart. — Lin Wei-cheng, Deputy Minister of Digital Affairs

Cable damage in the strait is not unusual. Taiwanese authorities recorded 27 separate cable faults in the preceding twelve months, the large majority attributed to fishing gear and anchor drag. Officials noted, however, that no previous incident had taken both Matsu routes simultaneously.

A specialised emergency session is due to convene at 09:00. A spokesperson declined to say whether the incident had been characterised internally as a hostile act.

Regional partners have not issued statements. The United States Seventh Fleet said only that it does not comment on force posture.

TWS has not independently verified conditions on Matsu. This story will be updated.

TAIWAN GOES DARK: STRAIT 'ON THE BRINK' AS INVASION FEARS GRIP ISLAND

OUTLET: signal (The Signal Post)

TIME: 2026-08-05T02:44:00Z

BYLINE: Signal Post Newsdesk

DATELINE:

TAGS: breaking

HEADLINE: TAIWAN GOES DARK: STRAIT 'ON THE BRINK' AS INVASION FEARS GRIP ISLAND

STANDFIRST: Communications blackout spreads as officials scramble. Western intelligence 'already knows who did it', source claims

BODY

Taiwan was plunged into a communications blackout in the early hours of Wednesday morning after a coordinated strike severed the island's undersea internet cables, in what analysts are calling the most dangerous moment in the strait for a generation.

At least four cables have been cut, leaving hundreds of thousands of people without any way to contact the outside world.

Western intelligence agencies already know exactly who is responsible and are preparing to go public within hours, a source close to the security services told the Signal Post.

A Taiwanese official, speaking on condition of anonymity, described the mood in Taipei as 'the closest we have come to war'.

Residents on outlying islands have reported hearing what some described as engine noise offshore in the hours before the blackout. The Signal Post has been unable to verify these accounts.

Naval vessels have reportedly been repositioning across the region overnight. There is no confirmation of the movements.

Global markets are expected to open sharply lower.

The Signal Post has approached the Taiwanese government for comment.
