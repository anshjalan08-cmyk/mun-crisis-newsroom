How to read this site

Meridian carries filings from several different outlets. They do not agree with each other, and they are not equally reliable. That is the point.

Before you cite anything in committee, check it against the Source Register. Ask four questions of every filing:

  1. Who is the source? Named, or "officials familiar with the matter"?
  2. Who owns the outlet? Who pays for it, and what do they want?
  3. Does it correct itself? An outlet that has never issued a correction has not been careful. It has been lucky, or it has not been looking.
  4. What is it not saying? The most effective way to mislead is to be entirely accurate about the wrong things.
